One-image summary of Thursday 19 November 2026: a single 1024×1024 infographic carrying 31 verified stories from five continents — 11 illustrated panels (the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, the ICJ finding Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide, GTA VI launching worldwide, Anthropic's record-breaking IPO debut, Denmark ratifying the Greenland security deal 109–0, the deadly Bangladesh lockdown, Israel ending its Gaza tank raid, the third Crimean Bridge strike in a week, the Coalition of the Willing meeting in Berlin, COP31 entering its final stretch and Cyclone Akvan forcing 80,000 evacuations in Odisha) plus a schematic "Where it happened" map and 20 further briefs. The design is an original deep-teal, warm-paper and coral newspaper layout with hand-drawn flat SVG scenes throughout. Every story was checked against reputable reporting dated to that Thursday — including an explicit day-check per story in SOURCES.md — and widely circulated wrong-day items (the GTA VI reviews and Metacritic 99, the Crimean Bridge road-span collapse, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan and the Iran strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain, all 18 November or earlier) were deliberately left out. The artwork lives in index.html and is rendered to entry.png; no external images or fonts are used.
